# SchemeSaathi (स्कीमसाथी) 🌾🏛️

> **Smart Welfare & Scholarship Discovery Platform for Maharashtra (MahaDBT) and Central Government Schemes** with verified eligibility rules, comprehensive benefits, interactive document checklists, and direct official application/registration links.

---

## 🌟 Overview

**SchemeSaathi** bridges the gap between citizens and government welfare schemes. Navigating multiple government portals (such as **MahaDBT Aaple Sarkar DBT**, **National Scholarship Portal (NSP)**, and **PM-KISAN**) can be confusing, especially with complex income cutoffs, caste validity requirements, and CAP admission rules. 

SchemeSaathi simplifies this with:
- **Direct Official Portal Links**: Instant one-click links to official registration pages and login portals.
- **Instant 1-Click Eligibility Check**: Live matching against family income, domicile, social category, and landholding.
- **Interactive Document Checklists**: Check off required certificates (Domicile, Income, Caste Validity, 7/12 extract) before visiting a Setu Kendra or CSC center.
- **Bilingual Interface**: Native support for English, Hindi, and Marathi.
- **Privacy-First Architecture**: Zero storage of personal identity (Aadhaar or bank credentials) — all screening logic evaluates locally in-browser.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 🔍 Comprehensive Scheme Directory
- Instant real-time search across scheme names, departments, keywords, and benefit descriptions.
- Multi-facet filtering by:
  - **Portal**: MahaDBT (Maharashtra), Central Government (NSP/PM), State Services.
  - **Support Area**: Higher Education, Agriculture & Farming, Women & Child Welfare, Pensions, Housing, Health.
  - **Social Category**: Open / EBC, OBC, SC, ST, VJNT / NT, SBC, Minority.

### 2. ⚡ Instant 1-Click Eligibility Checker
- Interactive profile builder:
  - Maharashtra Domicile status
  - Social Category / Caste Group
  - Annual Household Income Band (Under ₹1.5L, ₹1.5L–₹2.5L, ₹2.5L–₹8L, Above ₹8L)
  - Occupation / Role (College Student, Engineering/Polytechnic, Medical, Farmer, Woman, Senior Citizen, Divyang)
  - Agricultural Landownership (7/12 extract holder)
- Real-time categorisation into **Eligible / Aligned**, **Verification Required**, and **Excluded**.

### 3. 🔗 Direct Official Application & Registration Links
Every scheme card and detail page provides direct links:
- **New Applicant Registration**: e.g., [`MahaDBT Registration`](https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in/Registration/Registration/Register)
- **Direct Application / Login**: e.g., [`MahaDBT Applicant Login`](https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in/Login/Login) or [`MahaDBT Shetkari Login`](https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in/Farmer/Login/Login)
- **Official Government Resolution (GR) / Guidelines PDF**

### 4. 📋 Printable Preparation Notes & Document Checklists
- Interactive checklists where applicants can mark off available documents.
- One-click **Print / Save as PDF** or **Copy Checklist** to take directly to the Setu Kendra or CSC center.

## 🏛️ Supported Departments & Schemes

### 🎓 Higher & Technical Education (DHE / DTE)
- **RCSMSY (EBC - Higher Education)**: 50% Tuition & Exam fee reimbursement for non-professional degrees (BA, B.Sc, B.Com, MA, M.Sc).
- **RCSMSY (EBC - Technical Education)**: 50% Tuition & Exam fee waiver for Engineering (B.E/B.Tech), Pharmacy, Architecture, MBA, MCA, Polytechnic.
- **Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh Vasatgruh Nirvah Bhatta Yojna**: Hostel allowance up to ₹30,000/year for children of registered farmers / marginal landholders.

### 🤝 Social Justice & Special Assistance (SJSA)
- **GOI Post-Matric Scholarship for SC Students**: 100% Tuition & Exam Fee waiver + monthly maintenance allowance for SC students (Income $\le$ ₹2.5 Lakh).
- **Post-Matric Tuition Fee and Examination Fee (Freeship) for SC Students**: 100% Tuition Fee & Exam Fee reimbursement with **no upper income limit** (Income > ₹2.5 Lakh).
- **Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Merit Scholarship**: Monthly allowance for 11th & 12th SC students with 75%+ marks in 10th.

### 🏹 Tribal Development Department (TDD)
- **GOI Post-Matric Scholarship for ST Students**: 100% Tuition & Exam Fee waiver + monthly maintenance allowance for Scheduled Tribe students.
- **Post-Matric Tuition Fee Freeship for ST Students**: 100% Tuition fee support for ST students with family income above ₹2.5 Lakh.
- **Pandit Deendayal Upadhyay Swayam Yojana**: Cash DBT of ₹43,000 to ₹60,000/year for tribal students not allotted government hostel accommodation.

### 👥 VJNT, OBC & SBC Welfare Department
- **Post-Matric Scholarship for OBC Students**: 100% non-professional / 50% professional fee waiver + maintenance allowance (Income $\le$ ₹1.5 Lakh).
- **Tuition & Examination Fee Freeship for OBC Students**: Tuition fee waiver for OBC students with income between ₹1.5 Lakh and ₹8.0 Lakh.
- **Post-Matric Scholarship for VJNT Students**: 100% Tuition & Exam fee reimbursement for Vimukta Jati & Nomadic Tribes (VJ/NT-A, B, C, D).
- **Gyanjyoti Savitribai Phule Aadhaar Yojana**: Direct living & hostel allowance (₹38,000 to ₹60,000/year) for OBC, VJNT, and SBC students.

### 🌙 Minority Development Department
- **State Minority Scholarship (Higher & Professional Education)**: Up to ₹50,000/year or actual tuition fee for Muslim, Christian, Buddhist, Sikh, Parsi, Jain, and Jewish students.

### 🚜 Agriculture Department (MahaDBT Krishi Vibhag)
- **MahaDBT Krishi Yantrikikaran**: 40% to 50% capital subsidy on tractors, power tillers, rotavators, and seed drills via computerized lottery.
- **MahaDBT Micro-Irrigation (Drip & Sprinkler / Sinchan Suvidha)**: Up to 80% subsidy for small/marginal farmers on micro-irrigation installations.
- **Bhausaheb Fundkar Falbag Lagwad Yojana**: 100% financial assistance disbursed over 3 years for fruit orchard plantation (Mango, Guava, Pomegranate, Citrus).
- **Magel Tyala Shet-Tale**: Capital subsidy up to ₹75,000 for on-demand farm pond excavation and lining.
- **Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi Yojana**: ₹6,000/year Maharashtra State top-up for farmers (combined with PM-KISAN, total ₹12,000/year).
- **MahaDBT Pashusamvardhan**: 50% to 75% subsidy on milch cattle (cows/buffaloes) and goat rearing units.

### 🌸 Women, Social Assistance & Central Schemes
- **Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana**: ₹1,500/month (₹18,000/year) financial assistance for Maharashtra women aged 21–65.
- **Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Anudan Yojana**: ₹1,500/month pension for destitute, widows, and persons with disabilities (40%+).
- **Shravanbal Seva Rajya Nivruttivetan Yojana**: ₹1,500/month pension for senior citizens aged 65 and above.
- **PM-KISAN**: ₹6,000/year in 3 installments for landholder farmer families.
- **PMUY (Ujjwala 2.0)**: Deposit-free LPG gas connection, stove, and first refill.
- **PMMVY 2.0**: ₹5,000 for first child and ₹6,000 for second girl child maternity assistance.
- **Ayushman Vay Vandana Card**: Free cashless hospitalization up to ₹5 lakh/year for all seniors aged 70+.
- **PMAY-U 2.0**: Up to ₹2.5 Lakh subsidy / 4% interest subsidy for urban pucca houses.
- **CSSS (NSP)**: Central Sector Scholarship for college students above 80th percentile in 12th board.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React 19, TypeScript 5.9, Vite 7
- **Styling**: TailwindCSS 4, Custom Design Tokens
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **Architecture**: Monorepo with pnpm workspaces
- **State & Evaluation**: In-memory rule engine evaluating compound Boolean logic (`all_of`, `any_of`, `not`, `in`, `eq`)

## 💻 Getting Started Locally

### Prerequisites
- Node.js $\ge$ 20.x
- `pnpm` or `npx pnpm`

### Installation & Run

1. **Clone the repository**:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/rajsolankar31/saathi.git
   cd saathi
   ```

2. **Install dependencies**:
   ```bash
   npx pnpm install --ignore-scripts
   ```

3. **Start the development server**:
   ```bash
   npx pnpm --filter @workspace/scheme-saathi run dev
   ```

4. **Open in your browser**:
   Navigate to [http://localhost:5173/](http://localhost:5173/)

### Build for Production
```bash
npx pnpm --filter @workspace/scheme-saathi run build
```

## 🛡️ Privacy & Disclaimer

- **Zero Data Collection**: SchemeSaathi operates entirely within the user's browser session. No personal identifying information (PII), Aadhaar numbers, or bank account details are collected or stored.
- **Official Application Grounding**: All scheme guidelines and direct links are grounded in official Government of Maharashtra Resolutions and Central Ministry notifications.
