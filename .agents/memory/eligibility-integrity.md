---
name: Scheme eligibility integrity
description: Product safeguards for benefit-rule decisions, provenance, review, and sensitive data.
---

Eligibility decisions must be made by deterministic rules, never by an LLM. Each rule must retain its official source quote and verification date, and extracted rules need human review before publication. Results are informational, distinguish unknown from false, and explain uncertainty. Minimize sensitive data; never collect Aadhaar numbers; support deletion.

**Why:** Incorrect benefits guidance can deter people from applying or cause harm; scheme rules change and user circumstances are sensitive.

**How to apply:** Keep AI confined to parsing, question extraction, explanation, and translation. Require source provenance and reviewer approval when ingesting or refreshing rules; show caveats and unknowns to users.
